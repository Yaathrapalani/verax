# Research Claims Register

## Supported direction
- Heat-exchanger fouling prediction using ML is an established research area.
- RUL estimation and uncertainty-aware maintenance planning are established research areas.
- Cleaning optimization under uncertainty is established.
- The project therefore focuses on the decision layer between prediction and action.

## Intended contribution claim
FOUL-X proposes and evaluates a fouling-specific reject-option/reliability-gate decision layer with an explicit fixed-policy fallback, rather than claiming novelty for individual forecasting or optimization techniques.

## Claims we must NOT make
- "First system to predict fouling."
- "No existing solution can do this."
- "Guaranteed zero harmful maintenance decisions."
- "Production-ready for every refinery."
- "Industry validated" without documented evidence.
