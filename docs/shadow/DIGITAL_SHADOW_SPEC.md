# Digital Shadow Specification

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Stage 3 Baseline  
**System:** PLANT-X — Evidence-Gated Industrial Intelligence  

---

## 1. Problem & Purpose
The Digital Shadow is NOT a decorative 3D twin and NOT a full refinery simulator.

Its purpose is:
"Given all admissible evidence available up to time T, construct a provenance-backed, truth-state-aware, machine-readable representation of the plant and its observable state at T."

### Architecture Pipeline
$$\text{Industrial Evidence} \rightarrow \text{Stage 1 Intake} \rightarrow \text{Stage 2 Temporal Integrity} \rightarrow \text{Stage 3 Digital Shadow} \rightarrow \text{Time-Aware Snapshot (T)}$$
