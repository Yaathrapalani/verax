## Energy Balance Framework

Evaluates heat release and absorption terms across heat exchangers:
$$Q_{\text{tube}} = \dot{m}_t \cdot c_{p,t} \cdot (T_{t,\text{out}} - T_{t,\text{in}})$$
$$Q_{\text{shell}} = \dot{m}_s \cdot c_{p,s} \cdot (T_{s,\text{in}} - T_{s,\text{out}})$$
