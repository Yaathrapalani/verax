# Plant Workspace Specification

The `PlantWorkspace` is the central engineering viewport.

## Key Features
- **Shared Object Selection**: Selecting an asset (e.g. `E-102`) in the Plant Explorer tree or PFD highlights it in 3D and loads its full properties in the Engineering Inspector.
- **View Switcher**:
  - `PROCESS`: High-density schematic flow view.
  - `P&ID`: Piping and instrumentation topology drawing.
  - `3D`: Physical WebGL plant model with camera controls (Fit Plant, Orbit, Pan, Orthographic/Perspective).
  - `TRENDS`: Multi-sensor temporal trend charts.
  - `SIMULATION`: What-If counterfactual scenario workbench.
  - `EVIDENCE`: Causal lineage and hypothesis support matrix.
