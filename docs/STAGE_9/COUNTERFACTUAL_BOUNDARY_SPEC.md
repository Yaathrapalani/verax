# Counterfactual Boundary Specification

## Boundary Invariants
1. **OBSERVED \(\neq\) SIMULATED**: Counterfactual scenarios do NOT represent observed plant history.
2. **SIMULATED \(\neq\) PREDICTED**: Scenario calculations test hypothetical conditions; they do NOT replace baseline forecasting models.
3. **PREDICTED \(\neq\) DECIDED**: Scenario outputs do NOT issue autonomous maintenance commands.
4. **Stage 10 Separation**: Economic optimization and autonomous action belong strictly to Stage 10.
